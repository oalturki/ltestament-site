The Last Testament (the Noble Qur’an) — the seal of the heavenly messages

God sent Moses, peace be upon him, to the Children of Israel with the Torah — “the Old Testament.” After him He sent Jesus, son of Mary, peace be upon them both, with the Gospel — “the New Testament.” Then came the Last Testament, the Noble Qur’an, brought by Muhammad, peace and blessings be upon him, as the final message of the prophets. All of these messengers descend from Abraham, the father of the prophets: Moses and Jesus descend from Jacob son of Isaac son of Abraham, and Muhammad descends from Ishmael son of Abraham — they are cousins, all from the line of Abraham, peace and blessings be upon them all.

How does the Last Testament speak of the figures of the earlier testaments?

The same prophets — with details that may surprise you. Tap any card to read the verse.

Abraham, peace be upon him

Father of the prophets, and forefather of the messengers of all three testaments

From his descendants came Moses, Jesus and Muhammad ﷺ — so the bearers of all three messages trace their lineage to him. The Qur’an calls him “the intimate friend of God” (Khalīl Allāh), a title given to no other prophet.

Read the verse →

Ishmael, Isaac and Jacob, peace be upon them

Abraham’s sons and grandson — and Jacob is “Israel” himself

Ishmael helped his father Abraham build the Kaaba, and Isaac and Jacob carried the line of prophethood after him. Jacob is the one known as “Israel,” and his twelve sons are “the Tribes” (al-Asbāṭ).

Read the verse →

Moses, peace be upon him

The person named most often in the Noble Qur’an

His name, Mūsā, appears explicitly about 136 times — more than any other person. The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ himself is named explicitly only 4 times, even though the Qur’an addresses him directly, without naming him, in thousands of verses.

Read the verse →

David, peace be upon him

God gave him kingship, wisdom and knowledge

The brave warrior-prophet who brought victory to the Children of Israel by slaying their enemy, Goliath. God then gave him kingship and wisdom, and taught him what He willed — including certain crafts.

Read the verse →

Solomon, peace be upon him

He understood the speech of birds and ants

The wind and the jinn were made subservient to him, and he was taught the language of birds — and his full story with the Queen of Sheba is told in detail in the Qur’an.

Read the verse →

Mary, peace be upon her

The only woman mentioned by name — chosen above the women of the worlds

Of all the women in the Qur’an, Mary alone is mentioned by name (34 times), and a whole chapter bears her name — the angels themselves tell her that God has chosen her above the women of the worlds.

Read the verse →

Jesus, peace be upon him

Born of a virgin, he raised the dead by God’s permission

The Qur’an affirms his miraculous birth to the Virgin Mary, and that God worked great miracles through him: healing the blind and the leper, and bringing the dead to life — by God’s permission.

Read the verse →

Muhammad ﷺ

The seal of the prophets, and a mercy to the worlds

The Qur’an calls him “the seal of the prophets” — the last link in this long chain from Abraham to Jesus — and affirms that his message is not for one particular people but “a mercy to the worlds,” to all.
